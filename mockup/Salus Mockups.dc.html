Which TWO controls best reduce the risk of credential stuffing against a public customer portal?

AMultifactor authenticationYour answer · Correct
BPassword expiry every 30 daysYour answer · Wrong
CLogin rate limiting and bot detectionCorrect answer
DFull-disk encryption on web servers
Why

Credential stuffing replays leaked username and password pairs. MFA makes a correct password insufficient, and rate limiting slows automated replays. Frequent expiry doesn't stop reuse of a currently valid leaked password. Choose TWO questions score only when both picks are correct.
